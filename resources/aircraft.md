# Aircraft

Choosing the right aircraft add-on makes a significant difference to the quality and realism of your flying. This page recommends aircraft by category for both MSFS and X-Plane.

> Links on this page are provided for information only. VFO is not affiliated with any aircraft developer.

## Default aircraft

Both MSFS and X-Plane include a selection of default aircraft. The quality varies:

- **MSFS** includes well-modelled versions of the Cessna 172, Baron G58, TBM 930, and several airliners. The Fenix A320 and PMDG 737 exist as separate payware on top of the MSFS platform.
- **X-Plane 12** ships with the Cessna 172, Beechcraft Baron, Boeing 737-800, and others. The default 737 has improved significantly in XP12.

Default aircraft are a perfectly good starting point — especially for the early VFO airline ranks.

| Aircraft | Type | Source |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Cessna 152 Floatplane | GA float plane | flightsim.to |
| AT-AT Thrush 510G | Agricultural | flightsim.to |
| MilViz C310R | GA twin | flightsim.to |
| FlyByWire A32NX | Narrow-body airliner | [flybywire.ai](https://flybywire.ai) |

The **FlyByWire A32NX** is outstanding free software — it replaces the default MSFS A320 with a deeply modelled simulation, including a full FMGS, custom FADEC, and hydraulic simulation. Highly recommended for VFO airline flights.

## Aircraft by VFO rank

The VFO airline assigns aircraft by rank. If you want to pick an add-on that matches your current rank, see the [Fleet](../operations/airline/fleet.md) page for a full list.

For beginner ranks (Student through First Officer), the default MSFS GA aircraft and the FlyByWire A32NX are excellent free options. For higher ranks, PMDG or Fenix provide the level of depth that matches the complexity of those operations.
