# Peripherals

Hardware controllers add physical feedback to your inputs and significantly improve the experience of flight simulation. This page covers the main categories and some recommended products at different price points.

> Product recommendations are based on community reputation. VFO is not affiliated with any hardware manufacturer.

## Joysticks

A joystick is the most common first purchase for flight sim. It controls pitch and roll (and usually yaw via a twist axis).

| Product | Price range | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Logitech Extreme 3D Pro** | Budget | Reliable twist-rudder joystick, a popular entry point |
| **Thrustmaster T.16000M** | Budget–mid | Precise hall-effect sensor, left or right hand |
| **Thrustmaster HOTAS One** | Mid | Xbox/PC, includes throttle, good starter HOTAS |
| **Virpil WarBRD / VPC Mongoose** | High-end | Desktop gimbal, modular grip system |

## Yokes

A yoke replicates the control column found in most airliners and GA aircraft.

| Product | Price range | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Honeybee Alpha Flight Controls** | Mid | Good quality, included throttle, very popular |
| **Logitech/Saitek Flight Yoke** | Mid | Established product, wide range of add-on panels |
| **Brunner CLS-E MK II** | High-end | Force-feedback yoke, realistic load feel |

## Rudder pedals

Separate rudder pedals improve directional control, crosswind landings, and taxiing significantly. Any joystick twist axis is a poor substitute for proper pedals.

| Product | Price range | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Thrustmaster TFRP** | Budget | Entry-level, functional |
| **Honeybee Charlie Rudder Pedals** | Mid | Comfortable, smooth, widely praised |
| **MFG Crosswind** | High-end | Adjustable damping, very precise |
| **Virpil ACE-II Rudder Pedals** | High-end | Aluminium construction, hall-effect sensors |

## Throttle quadrants

| Product | Price range | Notes |

| **Honeybee Alpha** | Mid | Comes bundled with yoke, also sold separately |
| **Virpil THROTTLE CONTROL 3** | High-end | Fighter/military aircraft focused |

## Head tracking and VR

Moving your head to look around the cockpit transforms the immersion of simulation.

| Technology | Products | Notes |
| --- | --- | --- |
| **Head tracking (2D)** | TrackIR 5, Tobii Eye Tracker 5 | Maps head movement to in-sim view. Works with any monitor. |
| **VR headsets** | Meta Quest 3, HP Reverb G2, Varjo | Full 3D immersion. Requires a powerful GPU. MSFS and X-Plane both support VR. |

VR in MSFS and X-Plane is highly immersive but demanding. A mid-to-high-end GPU (RTX 3080 or better) is recommended for a comfortable VR frame rate.

## Connecting hardware

Both MSFS and X-Plane detect USB controllers automatically. Calibration and axis/button mapping is done in the simulator's control settings.

For complex setups, **FSUIPC** (MSFS) and **X-Plane's built-in mapping** handle advanced configuration including multi-axis calibration, button modifiers, and macro sequences.
