# Tools and software

A selection of third-party tools complement flight simulation and VFO operations. This page lists the most useful ones with a brief description of each.

## Flight planning

### SimBrief

[dispatch.simbrief.com](https://dispatch.simbrief.com) — Industry-standard flight planning tool. Creates a full Operational Flight Plan (OFP) including route, fuel, weather, NOTAMs, and weight/balance. Free. Essential for VFO airline flights.

See the [SimBrief guide](../guides/simbrief.md) for setup and usage instructions.

### SkyVector

[skyvector.com](https://skyvector.com) — Online aeronautical chart viewer with VFR sectionals, IFR en-route charts, and terminal procedures. Useful for VFR route planning and chart reading practice.

### Little Navmap

Free, open-source moving map, flight planner, and airport information tool. Works with MSFS and X-Plane. See the [Little Navmap guide](../guides/littlenavmap.md) for full details.

## Navigation data

### Navigraph

[navigraph.com](https://www.navigraph.com) — Provides current real-world navigation data (AIRAC cycles) for simulators and planning tools including SimBrief, Little Navmap, and most payware aircraft FMS. The subscription service keeps all your tools in sync with the same cycle.

A free Navigraph account is sufficient for SimBrief. The paid Charts subscription adds access to Jeppesen-style terminal charts.

## Simulator connectivity

### Little Navconnect

Free companion to Little Navmap. Runs on your simulator PC and broadcasts aircraft position data over your local network, allowing Little Navmap to run on a separate device while still displaying your aircraft.

Download from: [albar965.github.io/littlenavconnect.html](https://albar965.github.io/littlenavconnect.html)

### VFO Transmitter

VFO's own position broadcasting client. Sends your location to the VFO live radar so other pilots can see you in real time. See the [Transmitter overview](../operations/transmitter.md) for details.

### FSUIPC

[fsuipc.com](http://www.fsuipc.com) — A plugin for MSFS that provides advanced interface features: precise calibration, button assignments, axis curves, and a scripting interface. Used by many add-ons including the VFO ACARS client. Available in free and paid versions.

## Weather

### MSFS Live Weather

MSFS includes live weather injection from real-world data sources. Enable it in flight settings. No additional software required.

### ActiveSky (X-Plane)

[hifisimtech.com](https://www.hifisimtech.com) — Premium weather engine for X-Plane with METAR injection, turbulence modelling, and icing simulation. Payware. Used by many serious X-Plane pilots.

### NOAA Aviation Weather

[aviationweather.gov](https://aviationweather.gov) — Free real-world weather data including METARs, TAFs, SigMets, and PIREPs. Useful for checking weather at your route airports before flight.

## Add-on management

### MSFS Addons Linker

Free tool for MSFS that lets you store add-on packages outside the Community folder and selectively enable them per flight. Useful when you have a large library and want to reduce loading times.

Available on [flightsim.to](https://flightsim.to).

### X-Plane Organizer

Community tool for organising X-Plane plugins and scenery. Helps troubleshoot conflicts and selectively disable add-ons.
