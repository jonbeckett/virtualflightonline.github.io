# Code of conduct

Virtual Flight Online is a relaxed, supportive, and friendly community. Everyone is expected to treat other members with respect, regardless of experience level, simulator choice, or background.

## Expected behaviour

- Be welcoming to new members
- Keep discussions constructive and on-topic in relevant channels
- Offer help rather than criticism when someone is learning
- Respect other pilots' airspace and separation during group flights
- Follow any specific rules posted in individual Discord channels

## What is not acceptable

- Harassment, abuse, or discrimination of any kind
- Deliberate disruption of group flights or community events
- Sharing inappropriate content in community spaces
- Impersonating other members or VFO staff

## Age requirement

VFO facilities are intended for members aged **16 or older**.

## Reporting issues

If you experience or witness behaviour that violates this code of conduct, please contact a moderator via the [Discord server](../community/discord.md). Reports are handled with discretion.
