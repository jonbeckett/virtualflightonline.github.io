# Support VFO

Virtual Flight Online is a free community run by volunteers. If you enjoy being part of VFO and want to help keep the lights on, you can support us on Patreon.

[Support VFO on Patreon](https://patreon.com/virtualflightonline){: .btn .btn-blue }

Patron contributions help cover the costs of hosting, tools, and the infrastructure that keeps VFO running — including the transmitter server, airline platform, and this site.

There is no obligation to contribute. VFO is and will always remain free to join and participate in.
