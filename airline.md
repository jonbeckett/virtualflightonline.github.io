# Airline

The original VFO navigation links to a dedicated **Airline** site, showing that structured airline-style activity is an important part of the wider Virtual Flight Online ecosystem.

## What the airline section is for

This draft page acts as a placeholder for the virtual airline experience while the content is migrated into this site structure. It is intended to cover:

- airline participation and onboarding
- route ideas and event flying
- community standards for dispatch, planning, and reporting
- links to any dedicated airline systems or portals

## How it fits into VFO

The airline side complements the broader community by giving members a more structured way to fly together. It sits naturally alongside:

- the **Community** channels for coordination
- the **Operations** guides for procedures and tooling
- the **Codex** for reference material and shared knowledge

## Draft direction

As this site grows, this section can expand into:

- joining instructions
- fleet or route notes
- event schedules
- operational briefings
- recommended tools for airline flying within VFO

For now, treat this page as the landing point for future airline-related content within the GitHub Pages version of the site.
