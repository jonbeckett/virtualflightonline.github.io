# Upcoming events

Upcoming VFO events are announced and managed through the [Discord server](../community/discord.md).

The most reliable way to stay informed is to:

1. Join the Discord server at [bit.ly/virtualflightonlinediscord](https://bit.ly/virtualflightonlinediscord)
2. Check the **#events** and **#group-flights** channels
3. Enable notifications for the **#announcements** channel so you do not miss new posts

## How events are announced

Event posts typically include:

- Date and departure time in **UTC**
- Departure and destination airports (ICAO codes)
- Aircraft type or category
- Any special instructions or briefing links

Some events include a SimBrief link or a downloadable flight plan file.

## Proposing an event

Any member can propose a group flight. Post in the appropriate Discord channel with the date, time (UTC), airports, and aircraft. Even a casual two-sentence invite is enough.

See the [Event guide](../operations/event-guide.md) for more on how events work.
