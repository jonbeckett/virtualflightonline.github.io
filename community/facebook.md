# Facebook group

VFO has a Facebook group for members who prefer that platform. It is a secondary community space alongside Discord, used for sharing news, screenshots, and discussion.

[Join the Facebook group](https://www.facebook.com/groups/virtualflight.online){: .btn .btn-blue }

For day-to-day community activity and group flight coordination, [Discord](discord.md) is the recommended platform.
