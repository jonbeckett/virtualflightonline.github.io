# Discord

Discord is the heart of the VFO community. It is where members chat, plan flights, share screenshots, ask questions, and join voice channels to fly together.

## Joining

Use the invite link below to join the VFO Discord server:

[Join the VFO Discord](https://bit.ly/virtualflightonlinediscord){: .btn .btn-blue }

## Getting started

When you first join:

1. Read the **#rules** channel and accept the server rules to unlock the rest of the server
2. Introduce yourself in **#introductions** — let people know what you fly and your experience level
3. Browse the channels to get a feel for what is going on

## Voice flying

Many VFO group flights use Discord voice channels. A few tips:

- **Push to talk** is recommended to avoid transmitting background noise. Set a keybind in Discord's Voice & Video settings
- You can **share your screen** so others can see your cockpit or chart during a flight
- Set your **server nickname** to your callsign so other pilots can identify you easily

## Channels overview

The server includes channels for general chat, simulator-specific discussion, group flight announcements, tech help, and more. Check the channel list once you have joined for the current layout.
