# Newsletter

The VFO newsletter is published on **Substack** and is free to subscribe to. It covers community news, upcoming events, new features, and other updates from the VFO team.

[Subscribe on Substack](https://virtualflightonline.substack.com){: .btn .btn-blue }

Past issues are also available on the Substack page, so you can catch up on anything you missed.
