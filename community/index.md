# Community

The VFO community is active across several platforms. Discord is the primary hub for day-to-day chat, flight planning, and event coordination. The newsletter and Facebook group offer additional ways to stay connected.

| Platform | What it is for |
| --- | --- |
| [Discord](discord.md) | Real-time chat, group flight coordination, voice flying |
| [Newsletter](newsletter.md) | Regular updates and community news via Substack |
| [Facebook group](facebook.md) | Secondary community space for sharing and discussion |
