# Events

VFO events are how the community flies together. They range from casual drop-in sessions to planned fly-ins with routes, briefings, and voice coordination.

## Finding upcoming events

All events are announced and coordinated through the [Discord server](discord.md). Look for:

- The **#events** or **#group-flights** channel for upcoming announcements
- The **Discord Events** feature — scheduled events show at the top of the server and send reminders to members who RSVP

If you are not already on Discord, [join here](https://bit.ly/virtualflightonlinediscord).

## What to expect

Most VFO events are informal. There is no mandatory attendance, no strict dress code for your aircraft livery, and no penalty for arriving late or leaving early. The goal is to fly together and enjoy the community.

A typical event looks like this:

1. An announcement is posted with the airport, aircraft, and time (always UTC)
2. Pilots load in and join the event voice channel
3. A brief chat, then everyone departs together or in sequence
4. Voice coordination keeps the group together in the air
5. Arrival, screenshots, and conversation in Discord afterwards

For larger or more structured events, a full briefing document may be posted with routes, waypoints, and procedures. See the [Event guide](../operations/event-guide.md) for details.

## Types of event

| Type | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Informal fly-in | Someone suggests a flight, others join |
| Organised group flight | Pre-planned route with a set departure time |
| Airline tour flight | A VFO airline tour leg flown as a group |
| Themed event | Seasonal, commemorative, or destination-specific occasion |

## Proposing an event

Anyone can propose a group flight. You do not need to be a long-standing member or have any special role. Post in the appropriate Discord channel with:

- Date and time (UTC)
- Departure and destination airports
- Aircraft type (or open)
- Whether voice coordination is planned

Keep it short. A two-sentence post is enough to attract fellow pilots.

## After the flight

Share your screenshots and highlights in Discord. The community enjoys seeing the moments from each flight, and it helps build awareness for future events.
