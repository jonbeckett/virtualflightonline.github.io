# Virtual ATC

Virtual ATC refers to coordinated air traffic control during a VFO group flight, where one participant acts as a controller to sequence arrivals, issue traffic information, and keep everyone aware of what is happening in the airspace.

## How VFO handles ATC

VFO does not operate a formal ATC network. Instead, voice coordination happens on **Discord voice channels** during group flights. This is simpler to set up and works well for the scale of most VFO events.

Depending on the event, communication is handled in one of two ways:

| Style | How it works |
| --- | --- |
| **Open comms** | All pilots talk freely — useful for small informal flights |
| **Controlled** | One pilot acts as ATC and issues clearances — used for larger or more structured events |

The event briefing on Discord will state which approach applies.

## For pilots

Whether or not there is a designated controller, you are responsible for:

- **Announcing your position** when joining or leaving the pattern
- **Calling intentions** before any manoeuvre that affects other traffic (e.g. go-around, orbit, missed approach)
- **Acknowledging traffic calls** from other pilots so they know you have heard them
- **Maintaining visual separation** — do not rely on others to keep you clear of traffic

Basic calls on the event Discord voice channel:

| Situation | Example call |
| --- | --- |
| Entering the pattern | *"[Callsign], joining downwind runway 27."* |
| Turning base | *"[Callsign], turning base."* |
| On final | *"[Callsign], final runway 27."* |
| Going around | *"[Callsign], going around."* |
| Departing | *"[Callsign], departing runway 27, [direction]."* |

## For controllers

If you are acting as ATC for an event:

- State at the start of the flight that you are acting as ATC and on which frequency (Discord channel)
- Keep instructions clear and brief
- Give traffic information when it helps pilots: *"[Callsign], traffic is a Cessna on a 3-mile final."*
- Do not over-control — VFO flights are informal and pilots are responsible for their own separation
- Pass control to another pilot if you need to fly

## Network ATC (optional)

Some VFO pilots also fly on formal ATC networks where real human controllers provide full ATC service:

- **VATSIM** — [vatsim.net](https://www.vatsim.net) — the largest virtual ATC network
- **IVAO** — [ivao.aero](https://www.ivao.aero) — international virtual ATC

These require separate accounts and additional software. They are fully optional — VFO group flights do not require any network connection beyond Discord and the VFO Transmitter.
