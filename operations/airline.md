# Airline

The VFO virtual airline is a structured flying activity built on **phpVMS**. It lets pilots log flights, earn ranks, follow tours, and build a personal pilot record — all within the VFO community.

The airline is hosted at [airline.virtualflight.online](https://airline.virtualflight.online).

## What the airline offers

| Feature | Description |
| --- | --- |
| Routes and flights | Choose from a library of routes and log your flights |
| PIREPs | File a pilot report after each flight to record it officially |
| Ranks | Progress through ranks as your total flight hours increase |
| Scoring | Flights are scored based on smoothness, adherence to route, and other factors |
| Tours | Structured multi-leg flying programmes with specific aircraft and routes |
| Fleet | A curated selection of aircraft used on VFO airline operations |

## Getting started

1. Register for an account at [airline.virtualflight.online](https://airline.virtualflight.online)
2. Read the beginners guide on the airline site
3. Install the **ACARS** client to have your flights tracked and scored automatically
4. Pick a route and go flying

## ACARS

ACARS (Aircraft Communications Addressing and Reporting System) is the client software that connects your simulator to the airline back-end. It tracks your flight in real time and files your PIREP automatically on landing.

Full setup instructions are available on the [airline site](https://airline.virtualflight.online).
