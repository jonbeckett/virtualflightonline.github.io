# Beginners guide

This guide walks you through everything you need to get started with the VFO airline — from registering an account to completing your first logged flight.

## Register an account

Register at [airline.virtualflight.online/register](https://airline.virtualflight.online/register). You will be asked for:

- Full name (real first and last name)
- Email address
- Airline (choose any — aircraft choice is not restricted by airline selection)
- Home airport
- Country and timezone
- Transfer hours (if moving from another virtual airline — leave as 0 if not applicable)
- Password

After registering, a confirmation link will be sent to your email. Once confirmed, you will receive a pilot reference number in the format `VFA-0123`.

## Install ACARS

ACARS is the client software that tracks your flights and files your pilot reports automatically. See the [ACARS](acars.md) page for full installation instructions.

## Starting as a student pilot

New pilots begin at the **Student Pilot** rank, which restricts aircraft to basic trainers (Robin DR400, Cessna 152, Cessna 172). As you log hours and progress through the [ranks](ranks.md), more aircraft become available.

## Recording your first flight

### Starting a flight

1. Install ACARS following the [ACARS guide](acars.md)
2. Launch Microsoft Flight Simulator and spawn at a parking area with the aircraft powered off
3. Open ACARS and go to the **Flight** page
4. Fill in the flight details:
   - Airline: `VC - VFO Flying Club`
   - Flight number: any integer under 1000 for free flights
   - Flight type: Training
   - Departure and destination ICAO codes
   - Aircraft
5. Click **Start Flight**

### Finishing a flight

1. Land, taxi in, park, and shut down the aircraft
2. In ACARS, click **End Flight**
3. Click **Submit** to file the PIREP
4. View your flight on the [PIREPs page](https://airline.virtualflight.online/pireps)

## Types of flight

| Type | How to start |
| --- | --- |
| Free flight | Enter your own details directly in ACARS (use flight numbers below 1000) |
| Scheduled flight | Find a flight on the [Flights page](https://airline.virtualflight.online/flights) and send it to ACARS |
| Tour flight | Browse the [Tours page](https://airline.virtualflight.online/dtours) and send a leg to ACARS |

## Awards

As you file hours and rise through the ranks, you earn awards that appear on your pilot profile. See the [Awards page](https://airline.virtualflight.online/dawards) on the airline site.
