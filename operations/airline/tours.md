# Tours

Tours are curated sets of flights that take you around a particular region or theme. Each tour is made up of multiple legs that you can fly at your own pace. Your progress is tracked on the airline site — a green tick appears next to each leg you have filed a PIREP for.

Browse and fly tours at [airline.virtualflight.online/dtours](https://airline.virtualflight.online/dtours).

## Flying tour legs

1. Spawn at the tour leg's departure airport
2. Go to the tour page on the airline site and send the leg to ACARS (or enter the details manually)
3. Complete the flight and submit the PIREP
4. For tour progress to be tracked correctly, the airline, flight number, code, and leg must all match exactly — see the [PIREPs](pireps.md) page for guidance
