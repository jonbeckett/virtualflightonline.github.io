# Flights

There are three types of flight at VFO: scheduled flights, free flights, and tour flights.

## Scheduled flights

Scheduled flights are pre-planned routes based on a hub-and-spoke network. VFO has 7 hubs on each continent, with routes connecting hubs to each other and to over 600 spoke airports worldwide — approximately 6,000 flights in total.

To fly a scheduled flight:

1. Search the [Flights page](https://airline.virtualflight.online/flights) — you can filter by departure, arrival, airline, and aircraft
2. Either send the flight directly to ACARS, or use the Simbrief integration to prepare a flight plan first
3. Note that scheduled flights are tied to aircraft types — your [rank](ranks.md) determines which flights are available to you

## Free flights

You can fly between any of the 40,000+ airports in Microsoft Flight Simulator using any aircraft you are qualified for.

To record a free flight:

1. Spawn at your departure airport in a parking area
2. Open ACARS and enter your own flight details
3. **Keep free flight numbers below 1000** to avoid clashing with scheduled flight numbers
4. Flight numbers do not need to be unique for free flights

## Tour flights

Tours are curated multi-leg programmes. See the [Tours](tours.md) page for the full list.

To fly a tour leg:

1. Browse the [Tours page](https://airline.virtualflight.online/dtours) on the airline site
2. Send the leg to ACARS, or use the Simbrief integration
3. Your progress through each tour is tracked on the airline site — a green tick appears next to each leg you have filed a PIREP for

## Understanding flight numbers

### Scheduled flights

Scheduled flights are prefixed with a code for their airline sub-fleet, with flight numbers starting at 1001:

| Prefix | Sub-fleet | Aircraft |
| --- | --- | --- |
| VW | VFO World | Long haul — 747, 787, etc |
| VE | VFO Express | Commercial — A320, B737, etc |
| VB | VFO Business | Business jets — L35A, CJ4, etc |
| VT | VFO Transport | Turboprops — B350, SH33, etc |
| VH | VFO Historic | Heritage — DC6, DC3, etc |
| VC | VFO Flying Club | GA — no scheduled flights |

### Free flights

Enter any number below 1000 for a free flight. Do not use a code or leg number.
