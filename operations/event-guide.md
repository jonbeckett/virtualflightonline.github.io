# Event guide

VFO events range from a handful of pilots doing a quick hop together to larger organised fly-ins with pre-planned routes, briefings, and voice coordination. This page explains how events typically work and what to expect.

## Types of event

| Type | Description |
| --- | --- |
| **Informal fly-in** | Spontaneous — someone posts an invite on Discord, others join |
| **Organised group flight** | Announced in advance with a set route, time, and briefing |
| **Tour flight** | A leg from the VFO airline tour programme, flown as a group |
| **Themed event** | Special occasion events — seasonal, commemorative, or destination-specific |

## Finding events

Upcoming events are announced in the VFO Discord server. Check the events or group-flights channel for current announcements.

Join the [Discord server](../community/discord.md) if you have not already — it is the only place where events are listed and coordinated.

## Before the event

For an organised group flight, an announcement will typically include:

- **Date and time** — always in UTC; convert to your local time before the day
- **Departure and destination airports** — with ICAO codes
- **Suggested aircraft** — sometimes open, sometimes a specific type for the theme
- **Route** — some events publish a SimBrief OFP or share a PLN file
- **Voice channel** — which Discord channel will be used for voice coordination

Check in at least 15 minutes before the departure time. Load your aircraft, set up your flight plan, and join the voice channel.

## During the flight

1. **Join the voice channel** before departure — even if you do not speak much, being present helps the group stay together
2. **Spawn at the departure airport** — ideally at a gate or stand, not on the runway
3. **Wait for the go signal** — the event organiser or ATC will call when everyone is ready
4. **Set your transmitter group** to `VirtualFlight.Online` so you appear on the live radar alongside other VFO pilots
5. **Fly the briefed route** — deviations are fine, but call them on voice if they affect traffic
6. **Announce your position** in the pattern — see the [Virtual ATC](virtual-atc.md) page for call examples

## Formation flying

Some events involve formation flying. A few ground rules:

- **Follow your lead aircraft's movements** — mimic power and attitude changes
- **Maintain visual contact** at all times — if you lose sight, call it immediately and break away
- **Do not close distance rapidly** — approach slowly, stabilise, then hold position
- **Never overtake the lead** without a clear call and acknowledgement

Formation is difficult. If it is your first time, hold a loose trail position (directly behind, slightly offset) rather than a close wingtip formation.

## After the flight

- Post a screenshot in Discord — the community loves sharing moments from flights
- If you flew a VFO airline leg, submit your PIREP via ACARS or the manual form
- If the event was good, say so in Discord — event organisers appreciate the feedback and it encourages more events

## Organising your own event

Anyone can propose a group flight. Post in the appropriate Discord channel with:

- The date, time (UTC), and departure/destination
- Suggested aircraft (or "any")
- Whether you plan to use voice coordination
- A SimBrief link or PLN file if you have one prepared

Keep it simple. Even a two-sentence post is enough to get a flight together.
