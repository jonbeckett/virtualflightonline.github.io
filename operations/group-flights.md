# Group flights

Group flights are at the heart of the VFO community. They range from organised events with set routes and briefings to informal fly-ins where a few pilots decide to go somewhere together.

## Types of group flight

**Organised events** are announced in advance on Discord and typically include:

- a departure and destination airport
- a suggested aircraft type or livery
- a meeting time in UTC
- voice coordination in a Discord channel

**Informal fly-ins** happen more spontaneously. A member posts an invitation in the Discord server, others join as they wish, and the flight takes off when there are enough pilots ready.

## Simulators

Most VFO group flights are flown in **Microsoft Flight Simulator 2020 or 2024**. X-Plane pilots are welcome and can participate using the [X-Plane transmitter client](transmitter-xplane.md).

## Taking part

1. Join the [Discord server](../community/discord.md) to see upcoming flight announcements
2. Install the [VFO Transmitter](transmitter.md) for your simulator so other pilots can see you on the live radar
3. Watch for event announcements and reply to confirm you are joining
4. Show up at the briefed time and enjoy the flight
