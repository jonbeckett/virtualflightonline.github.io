# Community

Virtual Flight Online is more than a collection of tools. It is a place to meet other sim pilots, share progress, and keep up with community activity.

## Main community channels

| Channel | Purpose |
| --- | --- |
| [Discord](https://discord.com/servers/virtualflight-online-750398586851688499) | Real-time chat, coordination, and community discussion |
| [Facebook Group](https://www.facebook.com/groups/virtualflight.online) | Broader community updates and discussion |
| [Newsletter](https://virtualflightonline.substack.com) | Longer-form updates, announcements, and news |
| Forums | Referenced in the original VFO tooling and expected to return as the wider site migration continues |
| [Patreon](https://www.patreon.com/virtualflightonline) | Support the ongoing running and development of the project |

## How to get involved

- Join community discussions and introduce yourself
- Share your simulator setup and the aircraft you enjoy flying
- Take part in group flights and events as they are announced
- Help test evolving VFO tools such as the Transmitter ecosystem
- Suggest topics that should be captured in the Codex

## A good first route into VFO

If you are joining for the first time, a sensible path is:

1. join **Discord**
2. review the [Transmitter overview](operations/transmitter.md)
3. decide whether you want to fly through **MSFS** or **X-Plane**
4. keep an eye on the **newsletter** for updates as the site grows

## Supporting the project

VFO is a community-led effort. If you find the tools, guides, or events useful, Patreon support helps keep the work moving and gives the wider project room to grow.
