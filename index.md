# Virtual Flight Online

Virtual Flight Online is a friendly community for flight simulation enthusiasts of all abilities. Membership is free, the flying is fun, and everyone is welcome — whether you are a seasoned virtual pilot or just getting started.

The community is built around **Microsoft Flight Simulator** and **X-Plane**, with regular group flights, a virtual airline, live radar tools, and an active Discord server.
