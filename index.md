# Virtual Flight Online

**Virtual Flight Online is a free, friendly community for flight simulation enthusiasts of all abilities.** Whether you have never touched a simulator or have thousands of virtual hours in the logbook, you will find something here — and people who share your passion.

[Join the Discord](https://bit.ly/virtualflightonlinediscord){: .btn .btn-blue }  [Join the Airline](https://airline.virtualflight.online){: .btn .btn-purple }  [Live Radar](https://transmitter.virtualflight.online/radar){: .btn .btn-outline }

---

## A community built around flying together

VFO is built on **Microsoft Flight Simulator** and **X-Plane**. The community gathers on Discord to chat, plan flights, share screenshots, and fly together in organised group flights and informal fly-ins. It is relaxed, welcoming, and completely free to join.

Regular events mean there is almost always something to fly towards — a group flight to an interesting destination, a tour, or a fly-in that has been put together by a fellow member. You fly at your own pace, in your own aircraft, on your own schedule.

[Explore the community →](community/){: .btn .btn-outline }

## A fully featured virtual airline

The VFO virtual airline gives your flying a sense of purpose and progression. File flights, earn hours, and work your way up through **10 ranks** from Student Pilot to Class A Captain.

- **6,000+ scheduled routes** across a global hub-and-spoke network
- **40,000+ airports** available for free flights — fly anywhere, any aircraft
- **Tours** — curated route programmes across countries, mountain ranges, and continents
- **Automatic PIREP filing** via the ACARS client — no manual forms after most flights
- **Live scoring** tracking landing quality, airspeed discipline, and flight technique

Progression is measured in flight hours. Every filed flight counts. There are no fees, no subscriptions, and no minimum activity requirements.

[Get started with the airline →](operations/airline/beginners-guide.md){: .btn .btn-outline }

## See everyone flying — in real time

The **VFO Transmitter** is a lightweight desktop client that broadcasts your simulator position to the [live radar](https://transmitter.virtualflight.online/radar). Any pilot running the transmitter appears on the map in real time, so you can see who else is airborne, where they are going, and join up with them.

Setup takes a few minutes and works with both MSFS and X-Plane.

[Set up the Transmitter →](operations/transmitter.md){: .btn .btn-outline }
